Our story

From one neighbourhood to forty.

Ivan Property started in 2010 as a two-person agency focused on a single street. Today we're a full-team brokerage covering more than forty neighbourhoods — but the way we work hasn't changed. We still take time to understand what each client actually needs before we ever show a property.

Our agents live where they work. That means honest pricing guidance, sharp negotiation and advice rooted in real street-level market data rather than guesswork.
